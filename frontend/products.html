Good to know

Buying guide

Which size?

250 g is perfect for trying a new variety. 500 g lasts a household about a month. 1 kg is our best value.

Light or dark?

Lighter honeys (Moringa, Wildflower) are mild and floral. Darker Forest honey is bolder — great in tea or on curd.

Limited stock

Quantities shown are what's bottled right now. Seasonal varieties don't come back until next harvest.

Bulk or gifting?

For weddings, festivals or corporate gifts, add a note on the order form or WhatsApp us.
