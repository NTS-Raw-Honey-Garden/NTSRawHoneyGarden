Our promise

From our hive to your table — nothing in between.

We are not a brand that buys honey and puts a label on it. We are the beekeepers, and every jar can be traced back to a hive in our garden.

It may turn solid

Natural honey often sets into a soft, creamy state. Stand the closed jar in warm water for a short while and it flows again.

It changes with the seasons

Colour and flavour vary from batch to batch depending on what was in bloom. We think that's the point.

It's always ours

When a batch runs out, it is gone until the bees make more. We never fill the gap with honey from elsewhere.

Good questions

Before you order

How do I pay?

You don't pay online. After you place your order, we call or WhatsApp you to confirm, then you can pay by UPI or cash on delivery.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver locally ourselves and ship across India by courier. Delivery is a flat fee, and free on larger orders — the order page shows the exact amount before you submit.

My honey has turned solid. Is it spoiled?

No — this is natural. Stand the closed jar in warm (not boiling) water for 15–20 minutes and stir gently. Please avoid the microwave.

How should I store it?

At room temperature, lid closed, away from direct sunlight. Always use a dry spoon. Honey does not need refrigeration.

Is honey safe for babies?

Honey of any kind should not be given to infants under 12 months old. For everyone else, enjoy!
