Why our honey is different

Only coarse-strained

Locally harvested

Our promise

From our hive to your table — nothing in between.

Most supermarket honey is heated and finely filtered so it stays runny on a shelf for years. Ours is treated as the living food it is.

It may crystallise

That's normal for raw honey and a sign it hasn't been heated. Warm the jar gently in lukewarm water to make it runny again.

It changes with the seasons

Colour and flavour vary from harvest to harvest, depending on what was flowering. We think that's the point.

It's always ours

We never buy in honey to fill orders. When a variety sells out, it's gone until the bees make more.

Good questions

Before you order

How do I pay?

You don't pay online. After you book, we call or WhatsApp you to confirm, then you can pay by UPI or cash on delivery.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver locally ourselves and ship across India by courier. Delivery is a flat fee, and free on larger orders — the order page shows the exact amount before you submit.

My honey has turned solid. Is it spoiled?

No — crystallisation is a natural sign of raw honey. Stand the closed jar in warm (not hot) water for 15–20 minutes and stir. Please avoid microwaving.

How should I store it?

At room temperature, lid closed, away from direct sunlight. Always use a dry spoon. Honey doesn't need refrigeration.

Is raw honey safe for babies?

Honey of any kind should not be given to infants under 12 months old. For everyone else, enjoy!
