Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information NTS Honey Garden ("we", "us") collects when you use this website or place an order, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we need to deliver your honey.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
NameTo address your order and delivery
Mobile numberTo confirm your order and coordinate delivery (call / WhatsApp)
Email addressTo send your order confirmation and respond to queries
Delivery address, city, PIN codeTo deliver your order
Products, quantities, order value, dates, delivery preferences, your messageTo fulfil the order, keep business and tax records, and handle any issues
Marketing preference (optional)Only if you tick the box — to tell you about new harvests and offers

We do not collect card numbers, bank details, UPI PINs, government ID numbers, date of birth or any other sensitive information through this website. If online payment is added in future, it will be handled entirely by a licensed payment provider (such as Razorpay) and we will never see or store your card or bank details.

2. How we use it

  • To process, confirm, deliver and support your order.
  • To keep records required for accounting and tax purposes.
  • To contact you about your order (for example, if an item is out of stock).
  • To send occasional news and offers — only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

3. Consent

When you place an order, you give consent for us to use your information for the purposes above by ticking the consent box. Marketing messages need a separate, optional consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us; withdrawing does not affect orders already processed, but we may not be able to complete a pending order without your contact and address details.

4. Where your information is stored

This website itself does not store any customer information. When you submit an order, your details are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a private Google Sheet in the farm's own Google account, via Google Apps Script. Access is restricted to the farm owner and staff who handle orders. Google acts as our service provider for this storage.

5. Who we share it with

  • Delivery partners / couriers — only your name, phone number and address, to deliver your parcel.
  • Service providers — Google (data storage and email), and in future a payment provider, each only as needed to provide their service.
  • Authorities — only when required by law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as required for accounting and tax purposes (typically up to 8 years), after which they are deleted. Marketing preferences are kept until you unsubscribe. If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so unless we are legally required to keep it.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or update inaccurate information;
  • delete your information (subject to legal record-keeping requirements);
  • withdraw your consent, including for marketing messages;
  • address any grievance about how your data is handled.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, restrict access to order records to authorised people, protect the owner dashboard with a private key, and avoid collecting sensitive information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Cookies & analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. If we enable Google Analytics, it collects anonymous usage information (such as pages visited) to help us improve the site, with IP anonymisation turned on. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings.

10. Children

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.
